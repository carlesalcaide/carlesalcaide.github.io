## 👋 About me

I am a **Predoctoral Researcher in Computational Chemistry** at the **Institute of Computational Chemistry and Catalysis (IQCC)** at the **Universitat de Girona**. 

My research applies **quantum chemistry methods**, focusing on **excited states**, **aromaticity**, and **reaction mechanisms**. I frequently use Density Functional Theory (DFT) and Time-Dependent DFT (TD-DFT) to evaluate complex molecular systems such as Thermally Activated Delayed Fluorescence (TADF) emitters and cyclic acenes. Beyond running calculations, I actively write Bash and Python tools to automate data extraction, visualize chemical frameworks, and develop custom analytical software.

Before starting my PhD, I completed my undergraduate studies and MACMOM Master's degree at the Universitat de Girona. Outside of the computational lab, I am also a composer of traditional catalan sardanes!

---

## 🔬 Research interests

- ⚛️ **Quantum & Computational Chemistry**  
  Applying DFT and TD-DFT methods to understand molecular electronic structures and excited states.

- 💡 **TADF Systems & Optical Properties**  
  Evaluating the performance and photophysical mechanisms of Thermally Activated Delayed Fluorescence systems.

- 💍 **Aromaticity & Cyclic Acenes**  
  Running comprehensive benchmark validations and calculations on cyclic acene systems and assessing their aromatic character.

- 🔬 **Reaction Mechanisms**  
  Exploring complex chemical pathways, such as dimerization processes that proceed via stable intermediates rather than exclusively asynchronous routes.

- 💻 **Scientific Software Development**  
  Actively developing computational tools and writing scripts (utilizing `networkx`, `matplotlib`, etc.) to parse computational outputs and visualize chemical data.

## 📌 Featured projects

### 🖥️ The AROMA program
Active development of a specialized computational program designed to evaluate and analyze molecular ressonance structures.

### 💍 Benchmark validations of cyclic acenes
Large-scale computational benchmark calculations focusing on the electronic properties and structural behavior of cyclic acene systems.

### 🧪 Complex dimerization pathways
Mechanistic computational studies mapping out reaction pathways with pentalenes, successfully demonstrating that specific dimerizations pass through stable intermediates.

### 📊 Data automation & chemical visualization
Development of customized Bash scripts for parsing extensive Gaussian log files, paired with Python-based resonance visualizers using graph theory concepts.
