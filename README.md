## 👋 About me

I am a **Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow** and **Postdoctoral Research Scholar** in the **Amaro Lab** at the **University of California, San Diego**.

My research uses **all-atom molecular dynamics simulations**, **computational chemistry**, and **artificial intelligence** to understand how biomolecular systems behave, communicate, and evolve. I am especially interested in using the “computational microscope” to study **respiratory viruses in aerosol environments**, as well as enzyme dynamics, allostery, and biocatalysis.

Before joining UC San Diego, I earned my **PhD in Chemistry** at the **University of Girona**, where I worked on computational strategies to understand the molecular basis of biochemical and biocatalytic processes.

---

## 🔬 Research interests

- 🦠 **Virus dynamics in aerosols**  
  Molecular simulations of respiratory viruses, glycoproteins, mucins, and complex aerosol environments.

- 🧬 **Protein dynamics and allostery**  
  Network-based and dynamical analysis of communication pathways in enzymes and viral proteins.

- 🤖 **AI for molecular science**  
  Integrating machine learning and structural prediction tools with molecular simulations.

- ⚗️ **Computational enzyme design**  
  Mechanistic studies of enzyme variants, selectivity, catalysis, and biocatalytic engineering.

- 🌱 **Inclusive science**  
  Working toward more open, diverse, and collaborative scientific environments.

## 📌 Featured projects

### 🦠 Respiratory aerosol virology
Large-scale molecular simulations to understand how viral particles, mucins, proteins, ions, and water organize within respiratory aerosol particles.

### 🕸️ Dynamical network analysis
Development and application of workflows to extract communication pathways, shortest paths, and network changes from molecular dynamics trajectories.

### ⚗️ Enzyme allostery and biocatalysis
Computational studies of enzyme mechanisms, allosteric activation, selectivity, and engineered catalytic function.

### 🧪 VIDAR / molecular visualization
Exploratory workflows for molecular dynamics visualization and analysis, including work connected to VIDAR-based visualization tools.
